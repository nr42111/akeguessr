How to use this page

  • Use a north-up, unrotated map image. The conversion assumes latitude decreases downward and longitude increases to the right.
  • Pick two landmarks that are far apart and diagonal from each other, then click their exact positions on the image.
  • Enter each landmark's real latitude and longitude. You can find coordinates by right-clicking a location in Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.
  • Paste the generated bounds object into the matching sub-region entry in data/regions.json.
  • After adding all locations, run npm run validate to confirm the photos, maps, bounds, and coordinates are consistent.
